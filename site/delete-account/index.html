Delete Account

Options for deleting your ScoreIQ account and associated data.

Last updated 6 November 2026

Request deletion without the app

Email reodevelopers@gmail.com for your request.

The link opens your email application. Send the email to submit your request. You can also copy the address into your preferred email service.

Send your deletion request from your ScoreIQ sign-in email where possible and state that you want your ScoreIQ account and associated data deleted. Ownership must be verified before deletion. Keep passwords, verification codes, payment details and sign-in tokens private.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to the ScoreIQ account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings and select Delete Account. The option is also available from the app’s Privacy & Data controls.
  3. Read the confirmation, acknowledge that deletion is permanent and select Delete My Account. If prompted, sign in again and retry.

After you send a request

We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing deletion. If you cannot use your sign-in email, explain that in your message so ownership can be reviewed. Ask for confirmation of the outcome if you need to follow up.

What the deletion flow covers

The app removes your ScoreIQ sign-in account and starts cleanup of the cloud profile and synced study records linked to it, such as progress, bookmarks, mistakes and streaks. You can clear cached material by clearing the app’s storage on your device.

Security and deletion audit logs may be stored separately from the account profile. Ask about any retained information and its reason when making your request. Google Play manages its own purchase records. If you have a recurring ScoreIQ subscription, cancel it separately through Google Play Subscriptions before deleting your ScoreIQ account.

For access or correction requests, see Data Request.
