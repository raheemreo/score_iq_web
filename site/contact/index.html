Technical help and content corrections

If you have the app, open Settings and look for Help & Support or the Legal & Privacy Center. For a question correction, note the exam, paper and question number. For an app issue, note the app version, device model and steps to reproduce it.

Billing and account requests

For subscription management, visit Google Play Subscriptions. See Delete Account or Data Request for account and privacy options.

Keep passwords, verification codes, payment card details and sign-in tokens private.
