Data Request

Options for requesting access, correction or deletion of ScoreIQ personal data.

Draft for owner review. Prepared 6 November 2026.

Email reodevelopers@gmail.com for your request.

The link opens your email application. Send the email to submit your request. You can also copy the address into your preferred email service.

Send your request from the email address used for your ScoreIQ account where possible.

What to include in a request

  • Your ScoreIQ sign-in email.
  • Whether you are requesting access, correction or deletion.
  • Which data you want to access or correct, if relevant.

Ownership must be verified before account data is shared, changed or deleted. Keep passwords, verification codes, payment details and sign-in tokens private.

Other account options

You can update supported profile details in ScoreIQ. For account removal, see Delete Account. The Privacy Policy describes the information the app handles.
