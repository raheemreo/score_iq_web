In-App Purchases, Digital Coins, Subscriptions & Refund Policy

Section A — Digital Coins & Virtual Consumables

Coins are consumable digital items for use in ScoreIQ. You can earn Coins through eligible study activities or rewarded ads where offered, or buy Coin packages through Google Play.

  • Using Coins. Free users spend Coins to unlock eligible Practice Questions. Coins may also be used for other eligible in-app learning features. Active ScoreIQ Pro access includes unlimited Practice without spending Coins.
  • Coin value. Coins have no cash value and cannot be transferred between accounts, redeemed for cash or used outside ScoreIQ.
  • Reversed purchases. If Google Play refunds, reverses or revokes a Coin purchase, any balance adjustment applies only to Coins attributable to that purchase. Contact billing support if you need us to review a balance change.

Section B — Subscriptions & Premium Access

ScoreIQ offers free access and optional paid ScoreIQ Pro access. Recurring plans may be available on monthly, six-month or yearly billing cycles. The purchase screen shows the available plans, price, billing period and any promotional or renewal terms that apply to your purchase.

When you purchase an auto-renewing plan, you authorize Google Play to charge your selected payment method for each renewal period under the terms shown at checkout until you cancel. Lifetime Pro, where offered, is a one-time purchase with no recurring charge. Gifted Premium access follows the eligibility and duration of the grant.

Section C — Subscription Cancellation

To manage or cancel a recurring plan, open Profile → Subscription → Manage Subscription on Google Play in ScoreIQ, or visit Google Play Subscriptions with the Google Account you used to subscribe. Select ScoreIQ and follow the cancellation prompts.

Cancellation generally stops future renewals, and your Premium access normally continues through the current paid period. To stop renewal, cancel through Google Play even if you uninstall ScoreIQ or delete your ScoreIQ account.

Section D — General Refund Policy

Coin packages are generally non-refundable once credited to your ScoreIQ balance, subject to Google Play policies and applicable law. Refund eligibility for other purchases, including subscriptions, also depends on Google Play policies and applicable law. These terms preserve any consumer remedies available under applicable law.

To request a refund, follow the instructions in Google Play Help or use the relevant order in your Google Play order history. If an approved refund or reversal removes access associated with the purchase, ScoreIQ may update the affected Premium access or Coin balance.

Section E — Missing Purchases & Billing Support

  • Missing Coins. Check that the purchase is complete in Google Play and that you are signed in to the ScoreIQ account used for the purchase. If the Coins are still missing, contact billing support with your Google Play Order ID.
  • Missing Pro access. Use Profile → Subscription → Restore Purchase while signed in to the ScoreIQ account associated with the purchase and using the Google Account that made the purchase.
  • Billing support. Email reodevelopers@gmail.com with a brief description of the problem and the Order ID shown on your Google Play receipt, such as GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX. Keep passwords, verification codes and payment card details private.
