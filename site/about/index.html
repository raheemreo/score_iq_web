About Us

An Android study app for Kerala PSC and other competitive exams.

Built for exam preparation

ScoreIQ brings previous question papers, timed mock exams, topic practice and mistake review together. Learners can also use Quick Study Cards to revisit topics and browse current affairs in English or Malayalam where translations are available.

Our purpose

ScoreIQ brings practice and revision tools together so learners can work through exam material, revisit mistakes and build a study routine. The app offers English and Malayalam current-affairs content where translations are available, together with light and dark appearance settings.
