Privacy Policy

How ScoreIQ handles information in the Android app and on this website.

Draft for owner review. Prepared 6 November 2026.

Who provides ScoreIQ

Reo Technologies develops ScoreIQ, an Android app for Kerala PSC and other competitive exams. See Contact & Support for privacy inquiries.

Information the app handles

  • Account and sign-in. Depending on how you sign in, Firebase Authentication may process your account email, display name, profile image URL and user ID. Some guest features use local data or an anonymous identifier.
  • Learning and preferences. The app records study activity such as attempts, scores, mistakes, bookmarks, goals and streaks. It stores settings and cached study material on your device and syncs supported progress for signed-in accounts.
  • Purchases and access. Google Play processes payments. ScoreIQ uses purchase and entitlement information to grant or verify access, restore eligible purchases and manage virtual coins. Google Play handles payment card details.
  • App operation. The app uses analytics events and crash diagnostics to understand usage and diagnose problems. App Check helps protect backend services, and notification services support delivery of app notifications.

Services and advertising

Google Firebase provides authentication, data storage and sync, study content delivery, notifications, analytics, crash reporting and backend security. Supabase helps verify premium access, including eligible gifts, using Firebase sign-in and entitlement information. Google Play provides billing and distribution.

Google AdMob serves in-app ads on eligible placements. Its SDK may process device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived location, ad interaction and diagnostics for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where offered, you can manage ad privacy choices in the app through Google’s consent interface. You can manage notifications in your device settings.

Storage, retention and security

Local settings and cached data can be cleared through Android’s app storage settings. Synced account data is kept in cloud storage while needed to operate the account. Network connections use HTTPS and access to account records is controlled by authentication and backend rules.

See Delete Account for the scope of the app’s deletion flow and Data Request for access and correction. Google Play handles its own transaction records.

This website and updates

This promotional site uses static pages. It has no sign-in, advertising, analytics scripts or site-set cookies. The hosting provider may process standard request information to serve pages and secure the service. Links to other sites are subject to those providers’ policies.
