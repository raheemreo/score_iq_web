Privacy Policy

How ScoreIQ handles information in the Android app and on this website.

Last updated 6 November 2026

Who we are and how to contact us

ScoreIQ is an Android application developed by Reo Technologies for Kerala PSC and other competitive-exam preparation. This policy applies to ScoreIQ, Android package com.reotech.scoreiq, and this promotional website. For privacy questions or requests, email reodevelopers@gmail.com.

Information handled by the app

  • Account information. Google Sign-In or email/password authentication uses Firebase Authentication. Depending on the sign-in method, account information includes your email address, display name, profile photo URL and account identifier. Supported guest features may use an anonymous identifier or locally stored data.
  • Study activity. The app records information such as quiz attempts and answers, scores, mistakes, bookmarks, study goals, streaks and card progress to provide learning features and supported account sync.
  • Local storage and preferences. Downloaded study material, cached papers and cards, bookmarks and preferences such as theme and reminders may be stored on your device.
  • Purchase and entitlement information. Google Play processes payments. ScoreIQ uses purchase identifiers, verification information and entitlement records to manage eligible premium access, virtual coins and gifted access. Payment card details are handled by Google Play.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Analytics and diagnostic services may process app interactions, app and device information, installation identifiers, crash reports and performance information. Security services use app and device signals to help protect backend access.
  • Notifications. Notification services may use an installation or messaging token to deliver app notifications. Local reminder preferences are used to schedule study reminders.
  • Support correspondence. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send.

How information is used

We use information to operate your account, deliver study content, record learning progress, sync supported features, verify purchases and access, deliver notifications, answer support requests, investigate errors and protect the service against misuse.

Advertising and your choices

ScoreIQ uses Google AdMob for eligible in-app advertising. The advertising SDK may process device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising depends on applicable consent and provider settings.

Where available, use the app’s Manage Privacy Choices control to review advertising choices through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can also manage Android advertising settings and notification permissions on your device. Access to premium features and advertising availability depend on your current entitlement and the offer shown in the app.

Service providers and sharing

ScoreIQ uses Google Firebase for authentication, cloud storage and sync, study content delivery, notifications, analytics, crash diagnostics and app security. Google Play provides app distribution and billing. Google AdMob provides advertising. Supabase processes information used to verify Firebase sign-in and premium or gift entitlements. Email support is provided through Gmail.

Information is processed by providers as needed for these functions. Their services may operate in countries outside your country of residence. ScoreIQ does not sell, rent or trade personal data. Information may also need to be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Provider information is available in the Google Privacy Policy and Supabase Privacy Policy.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until removed through app controls, Android storage settings or uninstallation, subject to your device’s backup settings. Synced account information is retained while needed to provide your account and supported features.

You can request deletion of your ScoreIQ account and associated personal data in the app or by emailing us. The deletion process covers the account profile and associated synced study records, including progress, mistakes and bookmarks. See Delete Account for the steps and external request link.

Some operational records, such as security and deletion audit logs, may remain outside the account profile. Google Play and other providers manage their own records under their policies. If information must be retained for a legal, security or transaction-related reason, you can ask us to explain the category and reason as part of your request. Account deletion and Google Play subscription cancellation are handled separately.

Security

ScoreIQ uses authenticated access, backend access controls and encrypted network connections to help protect information. No method of storage or transmission provides an absolute security guarantee. Keep your account credentials private and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Your requests and controls

You can request access, correction or deletion of your personal information through our Data Request page or by email. We may ask for information needed to verify account ownership before sharing, changing or deleting account data. Keep passwords, one-time verification codes and sign-in tokens private. Available rights depend on applicable law and the request.

Younger users

ScoreIQ provides competitive-exam study material. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s use of the app or information associated with an account, contact us to request review or deletion.

This website

This static promotional website has no account sign-in, analytics scripts, advertising or site-set cookies. Images and fonts are served with the site. GitHub Pages may process request information, such as IP address and browser details, to provide hosting and security. Following an external link is subject to that service’s policies. Sending an email opens your email service and shares the information you choose to include.

Policy updates

Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. For questions about this policy or a change, contact reodevelopers@gmail.com.
